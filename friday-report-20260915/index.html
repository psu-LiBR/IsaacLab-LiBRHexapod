04 · Actions & Observations

Action space

Six binary leg commands: FR, FL, MR, ML, BR, BL. 1 commands stance and 0 commands lift, giving 64 combinations; masked PPO retains 24. The spine follows a Wave1 clock and is not controlled by RL.

Observation space

Policy: 32 dimensions = angular velocity 3 + projected gravity 3 + goal command 4 + joint position 8 + joint velocity 8 + previous action 6. The critic adds three body linear-velocity dimensions for 35 total. Policy observation noise is retained in training and disabled for evaluation.

08 · Next Steps

This round only performed coarse reward-structure and coefficient tuning; the coefficients have not yet been rigorously fine-tuned. The next round must keep the environment fixed, systematically fine-tune the coefficients around Candidates A / B, and use one-factor-at-a-time ablations to establish attributable conclusions.

01

Systematically fine-tune coefficients

The current coefficients are preliminary. We must search and fine-tune the progress, reach, stability, mechanical-cost, and action-switch coefficients around Candidates A / B.

02

One-factor-at-a-time ablation

Change only one reward term or coefficient per run, especially the strength of Candidate C's quality gate, to separate individual effects from bundled effects.

03

Log reward contributions

Record each reward term's cumulative contribution per episode and review it together with displacement, falls/torso contact, and video.

04

Check bugs and validate on hardware

Continue Jackson's requested code and evaluation-pipeline checks. After coefficient fine-tuning and ablation, test the stable candidates on the physical robot to determine whether the 0.21 simulation ranking transfers.
