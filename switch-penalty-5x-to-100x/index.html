Representative policy comparison

23 moderate-displacement + 10 higher-displacement candidates

Start with 0.6–0.8 BL/cycle. The separate >0.8 group preserves comparisons for Jackson to assess. Every list runs from smaller to larger displacement; the first row is not a universal winner.

Maximum command changes per individual leg per complete spine cycle: 2, 3 or 4. Zero recorded falls and torso contact. Straightness and command dwell guide selection; limitations remain visible. Available checkpoints cannot fill every algorithm/band.

Yaw/drift: maximum heading change and lateral deviation relative to the initial heading. Dwell: time between successive changes of the same leg command, not time without yaw. All measurements aggregate 64 simulated environments over 6 seconds; 5 complete phase-aligned cycles per environment determine the switch maximum.

Original only / original + added identify the two penalty experiments. Original weight = −0.005 × multiplier; added flip cost = 0 or 0.0004 × multiplier. Open a candidate for the full coefficients, files and copyable command.
